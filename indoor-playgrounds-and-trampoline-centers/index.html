Indoor Playgrounds and Trampoline Centers

It’s Las Vegas so you have to know we do things to the extreme. Why should our indoor playgrounds and trampoline centers be any different?

Search MLSCall (702) 362-8700

Scroll down to see a list of some of the more popular indoor playgrounds and trampoline centers in Las Vegas, Henderson, and North Las Vegas.

Flippin Out Xtreme

Flippin Out Xtreme is the largest trampoline park in Las Vegas and features many different activities. Trampolines, obstacle courses, laser tag, arcade games, and more. Featuring two Las Vegas locations with unique attractions at each spot. Flippin Out Xtreme also has large party areas and several different pricing packages for birthday parties of various sizes.

Full Access Pass 60 min- $16. 2 hr-$25. All-day pass -$30.

FlipNDeal $40 includes socks and a shirt. Kids under 4ft- 60 min $10. 120 min- $15. All-day pass $20.

Address: Summerlin Site: 245 S Grand Canyon Drive, Las Vegas, Nevada 89147.
Henderson Site: 1235 W Warm Springs Road, Henderson, Nevada 89014

Business Hours: Hours vary by day. Check their website for the latest business hours.

Website: Flippin Out Extreme

Giggles 'N' Tots Indoor Playground

Giggles ‘N’ Tots Las Vegas is designed to accommodate a parents’ needs of providing for their children a safe indoor activity center. Some of their features include giant indoor playground structure, spiral slide into a ball pit, large bounce house, indoor safety flooring, train table, soft mat toddler area, toys, reading nook, special monthly events for adults & kids.

Address: 4500 E Sunset Road #16, Henderson, Nevada 89014

Business Hours: Monday – Saturday 10 am to 7 pm
Sunday 10 am to 6 pm

Website: Giggles ‘N’ Tots

Jumper's Jungle

Jungle themed bouncy slides and obstacle courses with open play and party options. Jumper’s Jungle offers a variety of inflatable obstacles, slides, bouncers, and sports games. Safe and affordable, with one location currently on the west side of town.

Check out their website for party prices, multipass deals, and other discounts. Toddlers 2 & Under $7. Kids Ages 3 to 17 $10. Group Discounts Available. Specials Multipass-8 visits $60. Monthly Pass $35. Annual Pass $250. Families of Military, Fire, Police, Teachers, and Nurses receive a 10% discount. It cannot be combined with any other discount. You can check the calendar online to see exactly what is happening that day.

Address: 2050 S Rainbow Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89146

Business Hours: Mon – Sun 10 am – 6 pm

Website: Jumpers Jungle

Kangamoo Mega Indoor Playground

Kangamoo Mega Indoor Playground is a parent supervised children’s play center with an attached Parents’ Lounge and Healthy Cafe for children ages 1-10. Kangamoo offers young families in Las Vegas a quality indoor playground to encourage active play, that can be enjoyed all year round. The Parents lounge has free wifi and charging stations so parents can work or socialize while their children play. Private and semi-private birthday packages are also offered.

Walk-in Open Play $10per child (ages 1 – 10). Adults and young adults (11+ years old) accompanying children are always free. Infants under 12 months accompanying a paid child are free. Solo infants are $5. If you plan on going several times, they have a prepaid card for 10 visits that is $75 and a membership that is $250. If you enroll more than 2 children, you save 10% off of the second child. Members save 20% on party packages.

Address: 1525 E Sunset Road #7, Las Vegas, NV 89119

Business Hours: Mon – Fri 9 am – 7 pm.
Sat – Sun 9 am – 5 pm.

Website: Kangamoo Mega Indoor Playground

LOL Kids Club

LOL Kids Club is a large, densely built indoor playground that is a great option for entertaining children on hotter days or booking birthday parties. It features a full kitchen with surprisingly good food, many different play options for the kids, and special themed nights such as playground-wide foam dart gun wars. One of the largest indoor playgrounds in town, the layout of the facility also makes it excellent for parents to relax and unwind on their own as it is nearly impossible for a child to wander out of the facility unnoticed.

Price- 16.95 first child and 12.95 all additional children. Children under 2 are all 12.95 and adults are $5 per adult. You are able to book parties. Package prices vary depending on the amount of people/food choices.

Address: 7460 S Rainbow Blvd, Suite#130, Las Vegas, NV 89139

Business Hours: Mon – Sun 10 am – 9 pm

Website: LOL Kids Club

Skyzone

Skyzone is a chain of indoor trampoline parks which feature wall-to-wall trampolines, foam pits, obstacle courses, and trampoline dodgeball. It’s a surprisingly good workout, and appropriate for children of various ages and even most adults. They have an excellent platform for birthday parties, corporate retreats, and other large get-togethers which require ample party space.

GENERAL ADMISSION 60min- $16. 90 min- $19. 120 min- $20.

Address: 7440 Dean Martin Drive, Suite#201, Las Vegas, NV 89139

Business Hours: Hours vary – check their website

Website: Skyzone

Living At Los Prados, Nevada

Located in the southwest area of the Las Vegas valley, Los Prados is a 1,330-acre guard-gated master-planned community built around an 18 hole golf course designed by the well-known golf course architect Ted Robinson.

Amenities

Los Prados is defined by its golf course community. This 18-hole executive layout provides a perfect backdrop for many residents in the community who find themselves admiring sweeping views of rolling greens on their way home from work each day. The amenities at this course are top-notch with two swimming pools and recreational facilities that will keep you occupied all year round.

The Los Prados Golf and Country Club is an idyllic enclave near the city. The scenic atmosphere makes it a perfect place for those who want to escape from its bustling streets.

Dining

The Restaurant at Los Prados is the perfect place to enjoy a meal with family and friends. Either grab drinks in the 19th Hole Lounge or dine on seafood, steak, or prime rib served up by their experienced chefs.

Other dining options near Los Prados include the Charcoal Room, and the moderately priced Mezzo Bistro and Wine which was awarded a Tripadvisor 2013 Certificate of Excellence.

Shopping

Los Prados offers you a plethora of shopping opportunities. Costco Wholesale, Sears Outlet, Walmart Neighborhood Market, are just some of the major retailers to explore when checking out Los Prados’ area stores.

Health Care

Quality health care is an important decision for individuals and families choosing where to reside. Los Prados provides a convenient location for quality medical facilities nearby that span across many different specialties- from small private offices to large hospitals such as Canyon Medical Center, UMC Quick Care, Centennial Hills Hospital Medical Center, and Southwest Medical Associates.

Living At Summerlin, Las Vegas

The rich history of today’s Las Vegas real estate market is that Howard Hughes, Jr. acquired a 25,000-acre parcel in 1952 for $3 an acre as a possible relocation site for his company. He chose not to relocate to the land that sat dormant until 1980 when it was sold at public auction and became the Summerlin master-planned community.

Today, Summerlin is home to some of the most awarded Las Vegas neighborhoods in America. In 2002, it was named as its Community of the Year by Urban Land Institute and National Geographic Adventure ranked this neighborhood #1 for best places to live and play in 2007.

Amenities

In addition to its 150-mile network of trails and recreation options, Summerlin is home to dozens of parks that feature basketball courts, tennis/volleyball courts, baseball fields, and soccer fields. With all these amenities on offer in the heart of Las Vegas Valley’s most luxurious community – it’s no wonder why people are getting hooked!

The endless community and neighborhood parks in Summerlin spark energy, creativity, lifelong play, and a love of the outdoors. Plus many events each month are planned especially for residents to enjoy during the summer months!

Explore

More than 150 miles of trails are carved into this desert oasis, connecting Summerlin’s neighborhood villages. Today there are 10 miles of completed trail along with 215 Beltway and more on the way – eventually, this regional trail system will connect to Red Rock Canyon National Conservation Area.

Health Care

A person won’t have to go far in Summerlin if they want free access to a gym and fitness centers.

Staying healthy, active, and fit is a way of life in the city of Las Vegas where there are multiple health & fitness facilities including hospitals not to mention its very own #gym with day passes for just $10!

Frequently Asked Questions About Summerlin, Las Vegas

Is Summerlin, Las Vegas a good place to live in?

For those who don’t mind getting away from the busyness of downtown, Summerlin is a great neighborhood to live in with its beautiful homes and well-maintained parks. While it may be more expensive than other parts of Las Vegas and you’ll have to travel for good food, this upscale area has many perks such as safe neighborhoods that are not too far off the strip yet still offer plenty of amenities including grocery stores, shopping centers, and restaurants.

How much is a home in Summerlin, Las Vegas?

If you’re looking to buy a house in Las Vegas, the Summerlin area is an excellent place for your search. Homes there range from $165,000 up to as high as 14 million dollars.

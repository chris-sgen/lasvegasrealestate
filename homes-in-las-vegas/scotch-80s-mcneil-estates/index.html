Scotch 80’s and McNeil Estates, Las Vegas Homes For Sale

What was once old can be new again, but what is cool is cool and that doesn’t have to change with time. If you’re into living cool or being hip, or you just love the idea of “Vintage Vegas,” then the Scotch 80’s and McNeil Estates homes are where you want to go shopping for your modern desert property.

The Scotch 80’s

In 1913, Peter Buol, our first mayor, traveled to Scotland to find investors to develop and build homes on his 80-acre parcel of land in Las Vegas. World War II significantly delayed the project until the 1950s, but when it did take off, “Scotch 80’s” became the name of the community. Its close proximity to Downtown Las Vegas and The Strip made it a hit with celebrities, casino executives, and other cool people then and now.

Cool people then: Howard Hughes, Jerry Lewis, and Oscar and Carolyn Goodman

Cool people now: Brandon Flowers (The Killers), Rehan Choudhry (Life is Beautiful), and Pasquale Rotella (Electric Daisy Carnival), and Holly Madison.

McNeil Estates

McNeil Estates is a classic vintage neighborhood in central Las Vegas. It dates back to the 1940s, but most of the current homes were built in the 1960s. Large half-acre lots and single-story ranch-style homes are the main features of this vintage and incredibly charming Las Vegas neighborhood. These homes are as popular today as when they were first constructed!

Living At Scotch 80’s and McNeil Estates

Scotch 80’s and McNeil Estates homes are synonymous with Vintage Vegas! These classic neighborhoods are where you find our best examples of Desert Modern living. Step back in time to what was cool then and is still cool today.

Scotch 80’s

One of the city’s first master-planned communities, The Scotch 80s is a hip neighborhood in Las Vegas, where many of its young movers and shakers live.

The area has attracted casino titans, world-famous entertainers, and prominent Vegas power brokers over the years. But despite all the flash and cash that have come to define it as time went on; this small community still feels like a tight-knit town with friendly neighbors who are quick to welcome new people into their midst.

New, larger homes are popping up all over this upscale neighborhood that is home to past and present celebrities. Bordered by Charleston Avenue, Oakey Boulevard, Rancho Drive, and Shadow Lane near the Strip downtown freeways making it a prime location for someone looking to live in Las Vegas.

McNeil Estates

McNeil Estates is a classic vintage residential neighborhood located in Central Las Vegas. It dates back to the 1940s, but most of the current homes were built around 1960. The main features of this charming Las Vegas neighborhood are large half-acre lots and single-story ranch-style houses that remain very popular today as they did when constructed!

The affordability of homes in the area has contributed to its popularity, and all real estate listings are well-maintained properties. This neighborhood is not far from great schools as well as local amenities such as grocery stores, restaurants, parks, and more!

Desert Shores, Las Vegas Homes For Sale

Search MLSCall (702) 362-8700

Desert Shores wins the award for the most unique master-planned community and homes in Las Vegas, and perhaps that’s why it remains a popular choice for homeowners.

Located in the shadow of the foothills of northwest Las Vegas, this community was developed in 1988 and is incredibly rich in amenities. On a typical day, it’s common to see many residents out walking and enjoying the four man-made lakes out of their Desert Shores homes, there is also a lagoon-style swimming pool encircled by a sand beach, a picnic park surrounded by trees, playgrounds, and basketball and volleyball courts.

The community offers residents a 2,100 sq. ft. clubhouse, a great location for private parties and community events. With an emphasis on lakes and recreation, the Desert Shores, Las Vegas community offers miles of bike and walking paths and a beach facility that offers paddle boats and other fun water adventures.

Living At Desert Shores, Nevada

The Desert Shores Community consists of 3,351 homes and offers a variety of housing options. Located in the shadow of northwest Las Vegas’ foothills, this master-planned community was developed in 1988 and includes everything from condominiums to single-family homes with private gates and large lots.

The Desert Shores subdivision encompasses 682 acres and features four man-made lakes, a lagoon-style swimming pool surrounded by a sand beach, palm trees, and a picnic park with volleyball. The community docks provide paddle-boating and fishing for the residents while the 2,100 square foot clubhouse offers the opportunity for private parties or special events. With miles of walking paths throughout this recreation-oriented area, it’s no surprise that six days per week in spring through summer you can enjoy biking trails at Desert Shore as well as use their amenities such as kayaking on one of their lakes spots to explore nature up close!

Amenities

Desert Shores’ 200,000-gallon swimming pool is the perfect place to escape from reality and chill out. The water is filtered all year round and has a tropical lagoon feel that makes you forget about how miserable your day was. During the summer season, it’s open six days a week( closed only on Mondays for maintenance) with lifeguards on duty.

When the pool is closed, the Lagoon Park offers many different amenities for residents to enjoy. The basketball and volleyball courts, a sand beach with playground equipment, picnic pavilions that include barbecue grills, and play areas are all available in case you’re looking to plan an event or just get out of the house on those hot summer days

Dining

Along Regatta Drive, you will find several outdoor restaurants and wine bars. Among these are Marche Bacchus French Bistro – An upscale French bistro offering classic & contemporary dishes plus an indoor venue with lake views or their mezzanine patio overlooking the water.

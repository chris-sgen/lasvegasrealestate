The live host stopped accepting connections from the machine that read it on 6 October 2026, after 365 pages, and
has not answered that machine since (later attempts to reach it went unanswered). The copies in the rows after the first were read from the Internet Archive, which only gives
out copies it already holds; no request was sent to the live host to get any of them. The archive's copies of 38 more
addresses proved to be earlier addresses of pages that were read live, and were used only to tie those addresses to their pages;
3 more archived pages were read and not used (an old list of partner lenders, a partner the site's newer list no longer
names, a frame page of the listing vendor). 20 addresses of pages, 474 pictures and 1 stylesheet of the live site
have no copy there. The 18 pages of the live site that are not in this copy are 16 of those pages and the
2 pages set aside.

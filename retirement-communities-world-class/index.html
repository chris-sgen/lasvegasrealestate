Retire2LasVeas

The average state retiree population in the United States of America is 12.6%. In Las Vegas, NV the average percentage of retirees is expected to hit 19% by 2030. Why are so many retiree’s choosing Las Vegas, Nevada? Taxes, Healthcare, Things to Do, Cost of Living, World class retirement communities, an international airport, and weather are just a few very good reasons. Here is more information on each of these reasons to Retire2LasVegas!

Taxes

Nevada is very tax-friendly toward retirees. In fact, you will be hard pressed to find a better state for retirement based on taxation.

  • There is no State Income Tax in Nevada
  • Social Security income is not taxable. Social Security benefits, even those taxed at the federal level, are not taxed in Nevada.
  • Public Pension income is not taxed. Private Pension income is not taxed.
  • Withdrawals from retirement accounts are not taxed. Since Nevada does not have a state income tax, any income from a pension, from a 401(k), from an IRA or from any other retirement account is not taxable.
  • There is no inheritance tax in Nevada
  • Las Vegas (Clark County) Sales Tax is 8.25%
  • Property Tax: Assessed value is equal to 35% of the taxable value. Thus, if the Clark County Assessor determines your home’s taxable value is $100,000, your assessed value will be $35,000. Tax rates apply to that amount.
    • In addition, Nevada’s tax abatement law protects homeowners from sudden spikes in their property taxes.
    • While there are no general property tax exemptions for seniors, there are a few specific programs from which some retirees may benefit. The exemptions include a veteran’s exemption that is available to veterans who served in active duty during a recognized war period. There is also a disabled veteran’s exemption that is available to veterans with a service-connected disability of no less than 60%. The blind exemption is available to Nevada residents whose vision is no better than 20/200 when wearing contact lenses or glasses. The surviving spouse’s exemption is available to homeowner’s whose spouse has passed away and is equal to about $41 in savings.
  • Nevada Gasoline Tax is 33.52 cents/gallon
  • Diesel Fuel Tax is 28.56 cents/gallon

Retirement Communities

If you are looking for an active adult community in Las Vegas we now boast several of the finest 55+ retirement communities in the country. Las Vegas’ competitive cost of living, dry climate and numerous retiree activities are creating a lot of interest and our 55+ community is growing! Here are some of our better-known retirement communities but there are more, contact us for more information – we specialize in helping retiree’s move to Las Vegas – it’s one of our absolute favorite things to do because our clients that have moved here are very happy:

  1. Los Prados – A golf community in the NW area with several different age restricted communities to choose from though not all are age restricted. There are 22 villages and about half are age restricted. Amenities include a clubhouse, golf, two pools, walking trails, tennis courts and restaurant.
  2. Rancho Las Brisas – A 55+ community located in the Spring Valley area of Las Vegas near Spanish Trail Country Club. The community consists of 180 single-family homes for today’s active adults. Amenities include a clubhouse, multi-purpose room, library, catering kitchen, billiards, outdoor pool and two tennis courts.
  3. Promenade at the Meadows – Offering both townhomes and single-family residences this guard gated community offers 188 homes with amenities that include a clubhouse with hobby and game room that includes card tables, billiards and a kitchen. An outdoor pool and two shuffleboard courts round out the offering. All homes in the community are single-story typically with two bedrooms, two baths and a two-car garage.
  4. Solera at Stallion Mountain – A Del Webb 55+ retirement community located in Las Vegas just minutes from the Strip. Solera at Stallion Mountain is an intimate enclave of just 840
    homes with its own recreation center and guard gated entrance. Enclosed within Stallion Mountain Country Club this community boasts amenities to include a clubhouse, fitness center, outdoor pool and a multi-purpose room for hobbies and games including billiards.
  5. Solera at Anthem – A Del Webb 55+ retirement community located in Henderson, NV features community amenities to include a clubhouse featuring an indoor pool and spa, fully equipped fitness center, fitness classes, billiards, a computer room, card rooms, tennis and bocce ball outside.
  6. Siena – A 667-acre active adult community (over 50) located within the master planned community of Summerlin in Las Vegas, Nevada. Secluded behind a six-foot high masonry perimeter wall and guard gates are approximately 2,000 beautifully designed homes boasting some of the cities best amenities. Amenities include a ballroom, library, billiards room, health
    and fitness center, indoor and outdoor pools, a bistro and golf club.
  7. Ardiente – A gated active-adult community, conveniently located in North Las Vegas just twenty minutes from the heart of the Las Vegas Strip. 788 homes built by Centex Homes and Shea Homes. Amenities include a 20,000-square foot clubhouse, two resort-style pools and spa, a 5000-square foot state-of-the-art fitness center, group exercise room, billiard room, card and games room, business office, fireside reading lounge and a multi-purpose event and meeting facility with gourmet kitchen. Men and women’s lockers with showers and steam rooms, tennis, pickleball courts, putting and chipping greens, basketball and bocce ball provide exceptional recreation opportunities.
  8. Sun City Aliante – An active adult community within Aliante, a North Las Vegas master-planned community located almost directly north of the world-famous Las Vegas Strip.
    Amenities offered include a clubhouse, community center with conference rooms, ballroom, indoor heated swimming pool, sauna, spa, game and hobby areas, billiard tables, fitness center, and computer lab; a Sports complex with tennis, pickleball, basketball, bocce and horseshoes.
  9. Sun City Summerlin – Del Webb’s Sun City Summerlin is a 55+ community and part of the Summerlin master-planned community on the western edge of Las Vegas. Summerlin is
    comprised of nearly 20 separate residential villages spread across 22,000+ acres. Summerlin is woven together by more than 150 miles of trails and over 100 neighborhood parks. Summerlin is very popular here in Las Vegas and is known for its tree-lined streets, great shopping, top name restaurants and award-winning resorts, casinos and spas. Sun City
    Summerlin amenities include four clubhouses, indoor spa, exercise room, table tennis, card rooms, meeting and multi-purpose room. Outdoor amenities include an Olympic-size pool, softball field, tennis courts and bocce ball.
  10. Sun City Anthem – Del Webb’s Sun City Anthem is one of the nation’s premier master-planned communities consisting of 7,000+ single-story energy-efficient homes, some of which are situated on the community’s golf courses. All residents have access to three recreational centers that include two indoor and two outdoor pools and an indoor track, state of the art
    fitness equipment, tennis, bocce ball, pickleball, paddle ball, golf and on-site restaurant. Sun City Anthem even has its own TV station.
  11. Trilogy in Summerlin – Located within the master plan of Summerlin, the community’s convenient location, nestled on the western rim of Las Vegas, combined with its secluded
    desert feel, makes for a rare combination in premium 55+ living. The Resort Club is the centerpiece of Trilogy showcasing a variety of indoor and outdoor amenities including a rooftop deck, culinary studio, an unleashed dog park, a Zen garden, pickleball and bocce ball courts, fitness and movement studios, outdoor event lawns, and a resort and lap pool.
  12. Regency at Summerlin – This fun-filled, 55+ active adult, gated, community offers the best of everything from luxurious homes with majestic city views to spectacular amenities including an onsite lifestyle director. This fabulous master-planned community features three impressive home collections plus exciting resort styled amenities which include a 22,000- square foot clubhouse with indoor recreation including an indoor lap pool, state of the art fitness center, game rooms and a demonstration kitchen. Outdoor activities include pickleball courts, bocce ball, tennis and access to 150 miles of surrounding bike and walking paths.

We do have three months of triple digit heat (June – 99 degrees is close enough, July and August) but the rest of the year it’s hard to beat this desert community.

McCarren International Airport

The primary commercial airport serving the Las Vegas Valley with flights to just about every international and domestic destination you can imagine. McCarren served 48,500,000
passengers in 2017. Why are we telling you about McCarren as a retirement benefit to living in Las Vegas? It means you can fly to see your family as often as you like and it’s super convenient. The top ten domestic destinations out of McCarren include Los Angeles, San Francisco, Denver, Seattle, Atlanta, Chicago, Dallas, New York, Phoenix and Minneapolis.

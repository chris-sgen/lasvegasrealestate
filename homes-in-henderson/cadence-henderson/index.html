Cadence, Henderson Homes For Sale

Search MLSCall (702) 362-8700

Cadence, Henderson is a guard-gated community located southeast of Henderson near Lake Las Vegas and Lake Mead. Cadence is popular for a number of great reasons, but the 35,000 sq. ft. recreation center and Chimera Golf Club are two of the biggest hits with homeowners!

Cadence Recreation Center has to be one of the finest and most complete recreation centers built anywhere near Henderson residential areas or Las Vegas. There’s a full-size gymnasium that includes a basketball court, full-size tennis, and racquetball courts, and a complete fitness center that includes free weights, resistance training machines, and aerobic equipment. There are also two pools at the Cadence Recreation Center, a lap pool, and a resort-style pool. They even offer fitness classes!

If you aren’t into fitness, the recreation center also offers several arts and crafts classes, multiple meeting rooms, and even a few tables for a great family game of cards.

Meanwhile, the Chimera Golf Club is a privately owned Ted-Robinson-designed 18-hole public golf course. The 6,906-yard course offers golfers amazing views of The Las Vegas Strip and Sunrise Mountain. Chimera is quickly building a reputation for being a well-manicured and challenging course with great staff and delicious food on the menu—an all-around golf pleasure!

Living At Cadence, Henderson

At Cadence in Henderson NV, you’ll find a well-planned community that was carefully designed to provide all of your everyday conveniences. With 2200 acres and 450 open spaces featuring 10 parks with 30 acres of walking/biking trails overlooking peaceful mountain views, it’s clear why living here is such an appealing option.

The appealing homes in Cadence, Henderson NV are diverse and interesting. They offer single-family houses, townhomes, and condominiums built by CalAtlantic Homes, Richmond American Homes, Woodside Homes, and Lennar to appeal to different lifestyles.

Versatile features such as covered loggias that create an extended indoor-outdoor living area, patios, and balconies are all options. You can also enjoy a rooftop deck with views of the city.

Heritage at Cadence

Cadence’s master-planned community is home to a new active adult community, Heritage. This state-of-the-art recreation center features over 20,000 square feet of modern facilities including indoor and outdoor pools as well as an onsite fitness center for all residents ages 55 and above.

Recreation & Amenities

Cadence has a community that caters to all life stages and activity levels. For those who are active, or have families with busy schedules of practices and sports commitments, everything you need is pretty much in the neighborhood. If you’re looking for something more laid back than an active lifestyle find a bench in your favorite park where there’s free WiFI so you can watch the world go by while taking it easy.

Parks & Trails in Cadence

The 50-acre Central Park in the heart of your neighborhood is a perfect place to host special events and family gatherings. The community pool overlooks a 2,000 sq ft splash pad that’s sure to provide entertainment for all ages.

Meanwhile, Cadence’s 30 acres of walking and biking trails connect to regional trails, allowing you the opportunity to stay active while staying at Cadence.

Bike Share Program

The convenience of bike-sharing has a whole new meaning with the Cadence smart bikes. Have you ever thought about how cool it would be to ride around on your electric vehicle? From exploring streets and trails, navigating traffic jams in crowded city areas, or commuting through town for work – these bicycles are specifically designed to enhance mobility at any time as well as increase community connectivity throughout our big towns and small cities alike!
